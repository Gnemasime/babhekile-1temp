24 Years of Transformation Excellence

Babhekile Consulting Solutions evolved from skills development into a structured transformation advisory firm. We bridge strategy and execution by aligning people, systems, and processes for sustainable organisational impact.

  • Organisational change implementation and stakeholder alignment
  • Workforce transition programmes including flagship Bridge Training
  • Governance frameworks, compliance readiness and programme accountability

2002 – Sakha Skills Training Centre

Founded as a skills training centre focused on workplace readiness and development programmes. Laid the foundation for two decades of skills empowerment.

2011 – Sakha Skills Development

Expanded into comprehensive skills development initiatives and workforce transition programmes. Broadened impact across multiple sectors.

2018 – Sakha Consulting

Evolved into advisory services, offering organisational change and governance solutions. Strategic transformation became core focus.

2026 – Babhekile Consulting Solutions

Reimagined as a structured transformation advisory firm, bridging strategy and execution. A 24-year journey from skills empowerment to transformation architecture.

Ready to Transform Your Organisation?

Partner with Babhekile to design and implement workforce transition programmes, strengthen governance systems, and drive sustainable change. With 24 years of B2B expertise, we bridge the gap between strategy and execution.
